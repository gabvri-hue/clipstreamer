Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

ClipStreamer is used only by its owner. It does not collect data from any other person.

When the owner connects his TikTok account, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID and display name) via TikTok Login Kit. These are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used solely to upload the owner's videos as drafts. No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties other than TikTok.

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions).

Contact: via the GitHub profile of the owner (github.com/gabvri-hue).
